Platform Architecture

/01 Model Layer

Foundation Model Integration

Connect any LLM or custom model to your enterprise data. ONO abstracts model management so teams can experiment, deploy, and iterate without infrastructure overhead.

/02 Ontology Layer

Semantic Data Fabric

ONO maps your real-world operations into a live digital ontology — objects, relationships, and actions that AI agents can reason over and act upon in real time.

/03 Agent Layer

Autonomous AI Agents

Deploy purpose-built agents that monitor, decide, and execute across workflows. Agents operate within guardrails you define, escalating to humans when needed.

/04 Application Layer

Rapid Application Building

Ship AI-powered applications in days, not months. ONO provides the tools to build interfaces, automate decisions, and integrate with existing systems without code.

ONO doesn't replace your teams. It augments every decision-maker — from the boardroom to the factory floor — with real-time intelligence and autonomous action.

How ONO Works

/01

Connect Your Data

Ingest data from any source — databases, APIs, documents, sensors. ONO's ontology layer creates a live digital twin of your operations without moving data from its source of truth.

/02

Deploy Models & Agents

Select foundation models or train custom ones. Build AI agents that reason over your ontology, execute multi-step workflows, and learn from outcomes in production.

/03

Operationalize at Scale

Ship applications that embed AI into every workflow. Monitor performance, enforce guardrails, and scale from pilot to enterprise-wide deployment with full auditability.
