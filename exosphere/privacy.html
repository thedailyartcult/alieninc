1. Who We Are

Exosphere Succession Partners ("Exosphere", "we", "us", or "our") is a technology and M&A advisory firm within the Alien Inc group of companies, headquartered in Canada. Exosphere specializes in lower middle-market mergers and acquisitions, business succession planning, and exit strategy advisory for the $3MM to $30MM segment.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and process your personal data when you visit our website, engage our advisory services, or otherwise interact with us (the "Services").

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), the data controller is:

Exosphere Succession Partners
Data Protection Officer
Email: privacy@exosphere.tech
Canada

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

Data you provide to us directly

  • Identity and Contact Data: Your name, title, email address, phone number, postal address, and company affiliation when you submit an inquiry, register for updates, or engage our advisory services.
  • Business and Transaction Data: If you engage Exosphere for M&A advisory or succession planning, we collect business financials, ownership structures, transaction details, valuation data, and related documentation necessary to perform our engagement.
  • Communication Data: Correspondence you send us, including emails, contact form submissions, and records of meetings or calls with our team.
  • Preferences: Your marketing and communication preferences, including whether you wish to receive market insights, sector reports, or event invitations.

Data we collect automatically

  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, time zone setting, and platform type when you access our website.
  • Usage Information: Pages visited, time spent on pages, navigation paths, referring URLs, and interaction patterns on our website.
  • Cookie Information: Information collected through cookies and similar tracking technologies. For details, see Section 13 ("Cookies").

Data from third-party sources

  • Referral Sources: If you are introduced to Exosphere by an intermediary, accountant, or lawyer, we may receive your name and contact details from that party.
  • Public Sources: We may collect information from public registers, corporate databases, and news sources to verify business information during due diligence.
  • Service Providers: We may receive analytical and technical data from our website analytics providers.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and facilitate our M&A advisory and succession planning services;
  • To evaluate and process engagement requests and client onboarding;
  • To perform due diligence and business valuations;
  • To manage your client relationship and provide engagement reporting;
  • To communicate with you, including sending market insights, sector analysis, and event invitations;
  • To manage our website and improve user experience;
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including tax reporting and anti-money laundering requirements;
  • To enforce our terms of engagement and protect our legal rights;
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, unauthorized access, or other illegal activity;
  • To conduct internal analytics and research on market trends and client engagement.

5. Data Recipients and Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may disclose your personal data to the following categories of recipients:

  • Group Companies: Other entities within the Alien Inc group (including 1609 Holdings, Panteon, Statute & Precedent, The Daily Art Cult, and St. Alcantara Foundation) where necessary for consolidated reporting, strategic coordination, or operational purposes.
  • Service Providers: Third-party providers who assist us in delivering our services, including: website hosting, cloud infrastructure, email communications, analytics, legal and accounting services. These providers are bound by data processing agreements.
  • Transaction Parties: In the course of an M&A engagement, we may share data with potential buyers, sellers, their advisors, and financial institutions as necessary to complete a transaction, subject to confidentiality agreements.
  • Professional Advisors: Our lawyers, auditors, accountants, and professional consultants where necessary for the provision of their services.
  • Regulatory Authorities: Where required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Exosphere, our clients, or others.

6. International Data Transfers

Exosphere operates across international jurisdictions, with a focus on the Canadian lower middle market. When you access our Services from outside Canada, your personal data may be transferred to our servers or to servers of our service providers in other countries.

Where we transfer personal data outside Canada or the European Economic Area (EEA), we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Adequacy Decisions: Transfers to countries recognized as providing adequate data protection.
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): Approved under Article 46 of the GDPR, used for transfers to service providers in countries without adequacy decisions.
  • PIPEDA Requirements: For transfers from Canada, we ensure comparable protection through contractual arrangements or other reasonable means as required under PIPEDA.

If you have questions about international transfers, contact us at privacy@exosphere.tech.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. Specific retention periods include:

  • Client Engagement Data: Duration of the engagement plus 7 years (regulatory and professional liability requirements).
  • Transaction Documentation: 7 years from completion of the transaction.
  • Contact Inquiry Data: 24 months from the date of last interaction.
  • Marketing Preferences: Until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe.
  • Website Analytics Data: 26 months from collection.
  • Technical Logs: 90 days from collection.

When personal data is no longer required, we will securely delete or anonymize it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable data protection laws (including GDPR and PIPEDA), you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access (Article 15): Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification (Article 16): Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure (Article 17): Request deletion of your personal data, subject to our legal and regulatory retention obligations.
  • Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18): Request that we limit how we process your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability (Article 20): Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Article 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including for direct marketing purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@exosphere.tech.

9. Right to Withdraw Consent

Where our processing of your personal data is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us at privacy@exosphere.tech or by using the unsubscribe link in our communications.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. Where consent is withdrawn, we will cease processing your data for the relevant purpose, subject to any other legal basis that may apply.

10. Statutory and Contractual Requirements

Where we request personal data from you, we will inform you at the time of collection whether the provision of your data is a statutory or contractual requirement, or a requirement necessary to enter into a contract, as well as the possible consequences of not providing the data (Article 13(2)(e) GDPR).

For example:

  • Client Engagements: Business financials and ownership information are necessary to perform M&A advisory services. Failure to provide this data will result in inability to engage our services.
  • Contact Inquiries: Providing your email address is necessary to respond to your inquiry. Failure to provide this data will result in inability to respond.

11. Automated Decision-Making

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you (Article 22 GDPR).

Any analytical tools or market intelligence systems we use are designed to support, not replace, human judgment in our advisory engagements.

12. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest;
  • Access controls and authentication mechanisms;
  • Regular security assessments;
  • Confidentiality obligations for all team members;
  • Incident response procedures for data breaches.

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours and, where required, notify you without undue delay.

13. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience, analyse website traffic, and personalise content. Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website.

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function correctly. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website. These are only set with your consent.
  • Preference Cookies: Allow the website to remember choices you make.

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings or our cookie consent banner. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.
