1. Who We Are

KMT Consulting Group ("we", "us", or "our") is the strategy and applied AI consulting division of the Alien.Inc group of companies. KMT Consulting Group provides strategic advisory, applied AI workflow transformation, operating model design, and post-merger integration services to a global client base.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and process your personal data when you visit our website, engage our consulting services, or otherwise interact with us (the "Services").

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data for the purposes described below:

Data you provide to us directly

  • Identity and Contact Data: Your name, email address, phone number, job title, company affiliation, and professional background when you inquire about our services, subscribe to insights, or engage us for consulting work.
  • Engagement Data: Information provided during consulting engagements, including business documentation, operational data, strategic plans, and organisational information you share for advisory purposes.
  • Communication Data: Correspondence you send us, including proposal requests, meeting notes, and records of communications with our consulting team.

Data we collect automatically

  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and platform type when you access our website or client portal.
  • Usage Information: Pages visited, content engagement patterns, and interaction data on our website and knowledge resources.
  • Cookie Information: Information collected through cookies and similar technologies. For details, see Section 13 ("Cookies").

Data from third-party sources

  • Group Companies: Referral data and engagement context from other Alien.Inc group companies where we provide cross-group consulting services.
  • Public Sources: Publicly available business information, market intelligence, and professional network data used for research and client insight.
  • Service Providers: Analytical and technical data from our infrastructure and collaboration platform providers.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide, perform, and deliver our strategy and AI consulting services to clients;
  • To manage client engagements, project delivery, and ongoing advisory relationships;
  • To communicate with you regarding proposals, project updates, and service offerings;
  • To develop applied AI models and transformation frameworks used in client delivery;
  • To operate our knowledge engine, engagement tracking, and operational analytics;
  • To improve our consulting methodologies and service quality;
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including professional service and data protection requirements;
  • To enforce our engagement terms and protect our legal rights.

We process your personal data only for the specified purposes for which it was collected and do not use it for any incompatible purpose without further notice.

5. Data Recipients and Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share your personal data with the following categories of recipients:

  • Group Companies: Other entities within the Alien.Inc group (including Alien.Inc, Rousseau, Immanuel, Panteon, Centra, The Daily Art Cult, and Alcantara Art Foundation) for coordinated service delivery and cross-group client solutions.
  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting, AI platform, collaboration tools, and professional services infrastructure providers who assist in delivering our consulting services. All providers are bound by data processing agreements and confidentiality obligations.
  • Professional Advisers: Legal counsel, auditors, and compliance advisers where necessary to protect our legitimate interests or comply with legal obligations.
  • Regulatory Authorities: Where required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of KMT, our clients, or others.

When client data is shared with third-party service providers, we ensure contracts are in place that provide appropriate safeguards. We do not transfer client confidential information to any recipient without your authorisation unless required by law.

6. International Data Transfers

KMT Consulting Group operates globally, serving clients across multiple jurisdictions. When you engage our services, your personal data may be transferred to our servers or to servers of our service providers in other countries, including outside the EEA and outside the UK.

Where we transfer personal data outside the UK or the European Economic Area (EEA), we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Adequacy Decisions: Transfers to countries recognised by the UK Government or European Commission as providing adequate data protection.
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): Contractual clauses approved under Article 46 of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, which provide legally enforceable safeguards for your personal data.
  • Client-Specific Arrangements: Where required, we agree additional transfer mechanisms as part of engagement terms with our clients.

If you have questions about international transfers or the specific safeguards applied to your data, contact us at privacy@kmtconsulting.group.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. Specific retention periods include:

  • Client Engagement Data: Duration of the client relationship plus 7 years in accordance with professional service record-keeping obligations.
  • Prospect and Inquiry Data: Retained for 24 months from the date of last communication, after which it is securely deleted.
  • Technical and Usage Data: Retained for 12 months in identifiable form, then anonymised for aggregate analytics.
  • Marketing Data: Retained until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe, after which your data is removed within 30 days.

When personal data is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymise it. Data that must be retained for legal or regulatory reasons is stored in accordance with our retention schedule and kept for the statutory period required by applicable law.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable data protection laws, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access (Article 15): Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and information about how we process it.
  • Right to Rectification (Article 16): Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Erasure (Article 17): Request deletion of your personal data, subject to our legal and regulatory retention obligations.
  • Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18): Request that we limit how we process your personal data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Object (Article 21): Object to processing of your personal data based on legitimate interests, including for direct marketing purposes.
  • Right to Data Portability (Article 20): Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@kmtconsulting.group. We will respond to your request within the period required by applicable data protection law, and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

9. Right to Withdraw Consent

Where our processing of your personal data is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time. You may do so by contacting us at privacy@kmtconsulting.group or by using the unsubscribe link in our communications.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. We will honour your withdrawal of consent promptly and cease the relevant processing activity.

10. Statutory and Contractual Requirements

Where we request personal data from you, we will inform you at the time of collection whether the provision of your data is a statutory or contractual requirement, or a requirement necessary to enter into a contract, as well as the possible consequences of not providing the data (Article 13(2)(e) GDPR).

  • Client Engagement: Provision of contact data and engagement information is necessary to enter into and perform our consulting engagement. Failure to provide this data may result in inability to commence or continue the engagement.
  • Inquiry Submissions: Providing your name and contact details when inquiring about our services is necessary for us to respond. The consequences of failure to provide the data are that we may be unable to respond to your inquiry.

11. Automated Decision-Making

KMT Consulting Group uses applied AI and automated processes in its consulting methodology, including AI-assisted analysis, workflow modelling, and operational pattern recognition. These automated systems:

  • Do not produce legal effects concerning individuals or similarly significantly affect them;
  • Are used as advisory and analytical tools to support, not replace, human consultant judgement;
  • Are subject to human review by qualified consulting professionals for all client-facing outputs and recommendations;
  • Process data in accordance with client engagement terms and confidentiality obligations.

We do not engage in automated decision-making, including profiling, which produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you (Article 22 GDPR).

12. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures designed to protect your personal data, and the confidential client information entrusted to us, from accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure, or access. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256);
  • Client data compartmentalisation and access controls;
  • Multi-factor authentication for all consulting team access;
  • Regular security assessments conducted in partnership with Panteon;
  • Confidentiality obligations embedded in all consultant and subcontractor agreements;
  • Client data isolation protocols for each engagement.

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours and, where required, notify you without undue delay.

13. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience, ensure security, and analyse usage patterns. Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for website functionality, session management, and security. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our knowledge resources and service pages. These are only set with your consent.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences to improve your browsing experience.

You can manage your cookie preferences through our cookie consent banner or your browser settings. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.
