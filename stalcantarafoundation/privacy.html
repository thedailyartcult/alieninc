Privacy Policy

Last updated (modified): 2026-07-17 — effective date: 2026-07-17 (modified): July 2026

The St. Alcantara Foundation is committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, store, and process your personal information when you visit our website, book tickets, or register as a Member.

1. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for your personal data is:

St. Alcantara Foundation
Data Protection Officer
Email: dpo@stalcantarafoundation.org
Address: London, United Kingdom

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly to us when you execute specific actions on our site, including:

  • Contact information (such as name, email address, physical address, and telephone number).
  • Transaction details and billing information when reserving tickets, purchasing memberships, or donating.
  • Your preferences regarding news, events, and exhibition notifications.

Some data is required by law (e.g., financial records for donations) or necessary to fulfill our contract with you (e.g., ticket reservations). Failure to provide this data may result in inability to process your booking or membership.

3. How We Use Your Data (Legal Basis)

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under Article 6 of the UK GDPR:

  • Contractual necessity: To deliver digital tickets, confirm reservations, and process donations.
  • Legitimate interest: To manage and maintain your Foundation membership account.
  • Consent: To send newsletter updates if you have explicitly chosen to receive them.
  • Legitimate interest: To anonymously analyze website traffic to optimize user experience.

4. Your Rights

Under the UK GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 15): Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16): Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17): Request deletion of your personal data when it is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or when you withdraw consent.
  • Right to restrict processing (Art. 18): Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20): Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object (Art. 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

5. Right to Withdraw Consent

Where we process your data based on consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us at dpo@stalcantarafoundation.org or using the unsubscribe link in our communications. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

6. International Data Transfers

Your personal data may be transferred to, and processed in, countries outside the United Kingdom. Where we transfer data outside the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including UK adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses approved by the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected:

  • Membership data: Duration of membership plus 6 years.
  • Transaction records: 7 years (HMRC requirement).
  • Newsletter subscription: Until you withdraw consent.
  • Support inquiries: 2 years from resolution.

8. Automated Decision-Making

The St. Alcantara Foundation does not engage in automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you.

9. Right to Complain

If you believe your data protection rights have been infringed, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO):

Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Like most modern institutions, our digital platforms employ cookies to enhance functionality, analyze traffic metrics, and improve performance. This policy describes what cookies are and how we use them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information to the site owners.

2. Category of Cookies We Utilize

  • Essential Cookies: Critical for navigating the secure elements of our site, such as keeping you logged into your member account or retaining items in your shopping bag.
  • Preference Cookies: Used to remember choices you make (such as language selection or display settings) to deliver a personalized experience.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us measure visitor engagement anonymously to identify site issues and popular exhibits.
