What you can ask for

Under Section 8 of our Privacy Policy you have a right of access (GDPR Article 15) — a copy of the personal data we hold about you plus how we process it — and a right to data portability (Article 20): your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format. California residents have the equivalent right to know under the CCPA. That covers:

  • Identity and contact data — name, email, phone, company, role (Policy Section 2);
  • Communication data — inquiries and correspondence with us or any group company;
  • Technical and usage data — retained 12 months in identifiable form, then anonymised (Section 7);
  • Recipients and purposes — who we shared it with and why (Sections 3–5).

We respond within the period required by applicable law (usually within one month), free of charge, and will not discriminate against you for exercising this right.
