Your Privacy Choices

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information • Last updated: 07 October 2026

Our position, from Section 5 of our Privacy Policy: we do not sell your personal data to third parties. We share data only with group companies, bound service providers, professional advisers, and regulators as described in the policy — never for money, and never with data brokers.

Your rights

  • California (CCPA/CPRA): the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, and the right to limit the use of sensitive personal information.
  • UK / EU (GDPR Article 21): the right to object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests, including direct marketing — we stop unless we have compelling legitimate grounds.
  • Global Privacy Control: if your browser sends a GPC opt-out signal, we honour it as a valid do-not-sell request for this site.

Because we do not sell data, most visitors need take no action. Submitting the form below creates a verifiable record of your preference, which we apply group-wide and confirm by email.
