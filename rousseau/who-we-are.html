The House

Who we are

Rousseau Holdings is the capital and advisory pillar of an independent European ecosystem. We manage wealth for private clients, allocate capital across the group’s operating companies, and advise institutions on complex transactions.

Our approach is deliberately unspectacular: deep relationships, patient capital, and decisions designed to hold their value across generations.

Since our founding

Our history

The house grew in tandem with Monaco, combining direct regional proximity with long-term trust. From La Condamine, our story continues — a unique workspace open to new horizons where strategic decisions are designed.

Each chapter of our history shares one constant: capital deployed with human value in mind.

Stewardship

Governance

Oversight at Rousseau rests with an executive board supported by independent control functions spanning risk, compliance, and audit. Decisions follow a documented committee structure, ensuring accountability from first analysis to final allocation.

La Condamine, Monaco

Our headquarters

Our headquarters stands at the heart of the Principality — the permanent anchor of the house and the place where every strategic decision of the group is designed and approved.

We hire few, and we choose carefully. A career at Rousseau means direct exposure to decision-makers, mandates across private banking, asset management, and corporate finance, and standards that do not bend under pressure.

Speculative applications and responses to openings are reviewed centrally; candidates may route their enquiry through any of our offices.

Responsible investment

Sustainable development

We are committed to selecting investments that drive sustainable, structural growth. Responsible investment is embedded in how we analyse, allocate, and report — from site regeneration projects to the exclusion frameworks applied across our portfolios.

Sustainability reports are published annually within our reporting suite.

Corporate Finance

Corporate finance

Our corporate finance team advises owners and boards on transactions where discretion and certainty matter: disposals, acquisitions, restructurings, and capital raisings across the group’s core sectors.

External Asset Managers

Rousseau offers external asset managers institutional custody, competitive economics, and direct access to the house’s research and execution desks — a partnership model built on alignment rather than volume.
