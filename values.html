Principles

How we show up for each other and the mission

Each has a reason, a cost, and a way to tell we’ve broken it.

7 Values
01 / CONTINUITY

Build for the long emergency

We design for the worst year, not the best. Cost: slower growth.

Broken ifa critical system has no tested recovery path.
02 / VERIFIABILITY

Publish the bad with the good

Trust that can’t be checked is only a claim. Cost: unflattering numbers go public.

Broken ifa public number can’t be traced to the dataset, or a bad one is left out.
03 / GENEROSITY

Serve everyone we touch

Clients, artists, members, and the public all live with our work. Cost: we go beyond the contract and share the spotlight.

Broken ifsomeone’s work is used without credit or published terms.
04 / SOVEREIGNTY

Own the stack, raise the bar

A layer we rent can be switched off. Cost: we build what we could rent.

Broken ifa critical layer has one outside owner and no exit plan, or any company skips the shared baseline.
05 / SIMPLICITY

Ship simple, then harden

A simple version in the world teaches more than a perfect one in planning. Cost: we are corrected in public.

Broken ifa working version waits for perfection, or a shortcut is never hardened.
06 / INTEGRITY

Keep faith, repair fast

We assume good intent, speak plainly, and mend what breaks. Cost: hard issues get raised early.

Broken ifa rule is bent “just this once,” or a rupture sits unrepaired past one review cycle.
07 / STEWARDSHIP

Put the charter first

Every company, owner, and quarter answers to it. Cost: it outranks the founder’s preferences.

Broken ifthe parent overrides the charter, or cash moves outside an approved allocation.

The Seven Companies

Each company, and what it needs from the rest

Three values written for its own work, and a plain test for when it has slipped.

7 Companies
01 / SOFTWARE AND AI
Enterprise platforms

Panteon

Ontology-driven business operating systems, edge AI, and digital twins for the public, private, and nonprofit sectors.

Site
  • Map the business first
    Automating a bad model only makes the mistakes faster, so we model first and automate second. (Simplicity)
  • Own the intelligence layer
    We host and run the models ourselves. No client sits on a thin wrapper around someone else’s API. (Sovereignty)
  • Hand over, then stay honest
    A system isn’t done until it survives its first audit, its handover, and its fifth year, and we say so when it doesn’t. (Continuity)
Needs from the groupReal workloads from the sister companies, so the shared ontology is tested on live businesses, not demos.
Broken ifa client’s intelligence layer runs on a model we do not host, or a feature ships without a named owner.
02 / AEROSPACE INTELLIGENCE
Technical analysis

A-SAN

Asia's technical intelligence platform for advanced aerospace systems analysis and air superiority insights.

Site
  • Source every claim
    Each claim traces to a source, a platform, or a physical system. Hype isn’t analysis. (Verifiability)
  • Write for the reader
    The work is technical and the prose isn’t. If a reader needs a glossary, we rewrite. (Generosity)
  • Answer to no vendor
    No sponsor or manufacturer narrative shapes a conclusion, and better evidence can overturn any of ours. (Integrity)
Needs from the groupA firewall around its independence: the group never leans on A-SAN’s conclusions for commercial reasons.
Broken ifa published claim cannot be traced to a source, or a sponsor shapes a conclusion.
03 / CULTURE AND MEDIA
Independent publishing

The Daily Art Cult

Independent publisher and cultural media studio for membership, editorial series, and licensing.

Site
  • Pay the artist fairly
    Artists are collaborators and rights holders. Terms are public, fair, and credited. (Generosity)
  • Let members set the line
    Membership funds the work, so sponsors and advertisers never touch editorial. (Integrity)
  • Keep what matters
    What a civilisation keeps is what it chose to save. We publish as if someone will read it in a hundred years. (Continuity)
Needs from the groupPatience on growth, so membership and licensing can build without pay-to-play shortcuts.
Broken ifan artist’s terms are unpublished, or a sponsor influences coverage.
04 / CAPITAL
Holdings and allocation

Rousseau Holdings

Capital allocation, governance, board reporting, and portfolio operating discipline.

Site
  • Write the thesis first
    No capital moves without a written mandate, a memo, and a milestone. (Stewardship)
  • Compound, don’t force
    We would rather grow slowly than force a return that damages an institution. (Continuity)
  • Install controls early
    Boards, reporting, and controls arrive before scale, not after the first crisis. (Verifiability)
Needs from the groupClean, on-time numbers from every company in the one shared dataset.
Broken ifcapital moves without a written memo, a thesis, and a milestone.
05 / CRISIS AND RISK
Global response

Immanuel

End-to-end crisis and risk management: always-on intelligence, enterprise platforms, and in-country security and medical teams across 190+ countries.

Site
  • Be on the ground
    Coverage is measured in people in the country, not names on a partner slide. (Sovereignty)
  • Put the person first
    The person in the field outranks the commercial outcome. Always. (Generosity)
  • Stay calm and plain
    In a crisis we speak plainly, decide fast, and never trade in panic. (Integrity)
Needs from the groupCentra’s threat view and Panteon’s platform, so response runs on tools the group owns.

06 / SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE
Continuous verification

Centra

Automated security scanning, vulnerability assessment, bot defense, intrusion detection, and compliance monitoring for the entire group.

Site
  • Assume you’re already breached

  • Hold everyone to one bar
    The parent, the smallest company, and every vendor meet the same standard. (Sovereignty)

Strategy, applied AI workflow transformation, operating model design, and post-merger integration.

Site

How We Work

How the group runs

Seven rules for every company, board pack, and internal transaction.

7 Rules
01

One parent, seven operators

The parent sets standards, capital discipline, and disclosure policy. Each company runs its own strategy, team, and P&L.

02

One owner per function

Each function has exactly one owner, so no one has to guess who decides.

03

Everything in writing

Tenure, service agreements, and intercompany flows are written down. Disputes go to outside arbitration.

04

One source of truth

Every dashboard, board pack, and public number reads from alieninc-ecosystem.json. If it is not in the dataset, it is not a group number.

05

Independent audit

Centra scans every property and company against one baseline. No one is exempt, including the parent.

06

Disciplined capital

Rousseau allocates capital and reports to the board. Spending outside the plan needs an explicit decision, not a favour between founders.

07

Capability transfer

We measure KMT and Panteon by what clients can do without them. Dependency is not a business model.

Plain Terms

Words we use

The terms on this page that need a definition.

7 Terms

Parent

Alien Inc. It sets standards and capital discipline, and doesn’t run the businesses.

02

Operating company

An independent business with its own market, team, and P&L.

03

Ontology

A written map of how a business works, in a form software can read.

04

Source of truth

The one dataset every dashboard and public number reads from.

05

Tranche

Capital released in stages, each against a milestone.

06

Sovereign

Owned and controlled by us, not rented from someone who can switch it off.

07

Baseline

The one security and compliance standard every property and company is checked against.

Questions

Frequently asked

7 Questions
01

How do we know a company is keeping these values?

Every value and every company has a “Broken if” test. The dashboard reads from one dataset, and Centra checks every property against one baseline.

02

Can a company opt out?

No. All seven values apply to every company, and to the parent.

03

Why do we publish what each value costs?

A value with no cost is only a slogan.

04

Why does the parent hold itself to the same rules?

The standard only counts if no one is exempt.

05

How long is a “deliberate delay” on published numbers?

We haven’t fixed it yet.

06

What happens to the charter after the founders?

The succession and amendment rule isn’t written yet.

07

Where does ownership cost us a market?

Sovereignty means we sometimes decline work we could win by renting a layer. We haven’t written down where that line sits.

Seven companies. Seven values. The work outlives all of us.
