1. Who We Are

Statute & Precedent ("we", "us", or "our") is a legal services firm within the Alien Inc group of companies, established in 1976. Statute & Precedent provides legal counsel, compliance advisory, regulatory guidance, and dispute resolution services to the Alien Inc ecosystem and external clients.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and process your personal data when you visit our website, engage our legal services, or otherwise interact with us (the "Services"). This Privacy Policy does not apply to personal data we process on behalf of clients in the course of legal representation, which is governed by our client engagement terms and applicable professional conduct rules.

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR and EU GDPR), the data controller is:

Statute & Precedent
Data Protection Officer
Email: privacy@statuteprecedent.com
London, United Kingdom

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

Data you provide to us directly

  • Identity and Contact Data: Your name, title, email address, phone number, postal address, professional affiliation, and bar registration details (if applicable) when you engage our services, submit an inquiry, or register for updates.
  • Client Engagement Data: If you engage Statute & Precedent for legal services, we collect case documentation, correspondence, contractual information, and other materials necessary to perform our engagement. This data is subject to legal professional privilege.
  • Communication Data: Correspondence you send us, including emails, contact form submissions, and records of meetings or calls with our legal team.
  • Preferences: Your marketing and communication preferences, including whether you wish to receive legal updates, case law summaries, or event invitations.

Data we collect automatically

  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, time zone setting, and platform type when you access our website.
  • Usage Information: Pages visited, time spent on pages, navigation paths, referring URLs, and interaction patterns on our website.
  • Cookie Information: Information collected through cookies and similar tracking technologies. For details, see Section 14 ("Cookies").

Data from third-party sources

  • Court and Regulatory Databases: We may collect information from public court records, regulatory filings, and legal databases as necessary for case preparation and legal research.
  • Referral Sources: If you are introduced to us by another party, we may receive your name and contact details from that source.
  • Service Providers: We may receive analytical and technical data from our website analytics providers.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and facilitate the legal services offered by Statute & Precedent;
  • To evaluate and process engagement requests and client onboarding;
  • To perform legal research, case analysis, and regulatory compliance work;
  • To manage your client relationship and provide matter updates;
  • To communicate with you, including sending legal updates, case law summaries, and event invitations;
  • To manage our website and improve user experience;
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including professional conduct rules, anti-money laundering requirements, and court orders;
  • To enforce our terms of engagement and protect our legal rights;
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, unauthorized access, or other illegal activity;
  • To maintain our precedents database and legal knowledge base (in anonymized form).

5. Data Recipients and Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may disclose your personal data to the following categories of recipients:

  • Group Companies: Other entities within the Alien Inc group (including 1609 Holdings, Exosphere, Hawksight, The Daily Art Cult, and St. Alcantara Foundation) where necessary for coordinated legal advisory or compliance purposes. Data shared with group companies remains subject to legal professional privilege where applicable.
  • Courts and Tribunals: Where necessary for the conduct of legal proceedings on your behalf.
  • Opposing Parties and Their Advisors: In the course of litigation or transactional work, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.
  • Regulatory Authorities: Where required by law, professional conduct rules, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Statute & Precedent, our clients, or others.
  • Professional Advisors: Our auditors, insurers, and professional consultants where necessary for the provision of their services.
  • Service Providers: Third-party providers who assist us in operating our business, including: document management, secure communications, cloud hosting, and analytics. These providers are bound by data processing agreements.

6. International Data Transfers

Statute & Precedent operates across international jurisdictions, with offices in London and Tokyo. When you access our Services from outside the United Kingdom, your personal data may be transferred to our servers or to servers of our service providers in other countries.

Where we transfer personal data outside the UK or the European Economic Area (EEA), we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Adequacy Decisions: Transfers to countries that the UK Secretary of State or the European Commission has determined provide an adequate level of data protection.
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): The International Data Transfer Addendum or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, as applicable.
  • Legal Professional Privilege: Data subject to legal professional privilege is transferred with appropriate protections to maintain the privileged nature of the communication.

If you have questions about international transfers, contact us at privacy@statuteprecedent.com.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law and professional conduct rules. Specific retention periods include:

  • Matter Files: 7 years from the conclusion of a matter, in accordance with regulatory requirements and professional conduct rules.
  • KYC/AML Documentation: 5 years from the end of the business relationship, in accordance with the Money Laundering Regulations 2017.
  • Contact Inquiry Data: 24 months from the date of last interaction.
  • Marketing Preferences: Until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe.
  • Website Analytics Data: 26 months from collection.
  • Communication Records: 6 years from the date of communication.

When personal data is no longer required, we will securely delete or anonymize it. Data subject to legal professional privilege will be destroyed in accordance with our document retention policy and professional obligations.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable data protection laws, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access (Article 15): Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification (Article 16): Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure (Article 17): Request deletion of your personal data, subject to our legal and regulatory retention obligations and professional conduct requirements.
  • Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18): Request that we limit how we process your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability (Article 20): Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Article 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including for direct marketing purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@statuteprecedent.com. Please note that certain rights may be limited where data is subject to legal professional privilege or regulatory retention requirements.

9. Right to Withdraw Consent

Where our processing of your personal data is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us at privacy@statuteprecedent.com or by using the unsubscribe link in our communications.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

10. Statutory and Contractual Requirements

Where we request personal data from you, we will inform you at the time of collection whether the provision of your data is a statutory or contractual requirement, or a requirement necessary to enter into a contract, as well as the possible consequences of not providing the data (Article 13(2)(e) GDPR).

  • Client Engagements: Engagement documentation and identity verification are required to establish a client relationship. Failure to provide this data will result in inability to engage our services.
  • Contact Inquiries: Providing your email address is necessary to respond to your inquiry. Failure to provide this data will result in inability to respond.

11. Automated Decision-Making

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you (Article 22 GDPR).

Any legal research tools or AI-assisted analysis systems we use are designed to support, not replace, professional legal judgment and are subject to oversight by qualified solicitors.

12. Confidentiality and Legal Professional Privilege

Statute & Precedent is bound by strict duties of confidentiality under the Solicitors Act 1974, the SRA Standards and Regulations, and common law. Communications between you and our legal team in the course of a legal engagement may be subject to legal professional privilege (legal advice privilege and/or litigation privilege).

We will not disclose privileged communications without your express consent, except where required by law or professional conduct rules. Data subject to privilege retains its privileged status regardless of the medium in which it is held.

13. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest;
  • Access controls and multi-factor authentication;
  • Secure document management systems;
  • Regular security assessments and penetration testing;
  • Confidentiality obligations for all team members;
  • Incident response procedures for data breaches.

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours and, where required, notify you without undue delay.

14. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience, analyse website traffic, and personalise content. Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website.

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function correctly. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website. These are only set with your consent.
  • Preference Cookies: Allow the website to remember choices you make.

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings or our cookie consent banner. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.
