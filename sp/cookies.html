Cookies Policy

Last updated (modified): 14 July 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help us understand how you use our site and improve your experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: required for the website to function properly.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand how visitors interact with our site.
  • Preference cookies: remember your settings and preferences.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Session Maintain user session state End of session
Analytics Anonymous usage statistics 12 months
Preferences Remember user settings 12 months

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set cookies on your device, including analytics and hosting providers. These third parties have their own privacy policies.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies. Note: blocking essential cookies may impair website functionality.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this cookies policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
