The Discipline of Origin

Etiology is the discipline of origin. It continuously reconstructs the full causal graph of the environment: the chains of decisions, logistics movements, influence operations, equipment states, human relationships, and environmental factors that produced the present moment.

Every significant event is treated as a node with ancestry. Every anomaly is traced through multi-hop causal paths. Alternative histories are generated and ranked. Confidence is quantified. Gaps in the graph are surfaced rather than ignored.

The result is digital forensics elevated from after-action review into continuous, real-time operational capability. Commanders and analysts no longer ask only "what occurred?" They ask, and receive structured answers to, "what sequence of causes made this inevitable, what interventions would have altered the path, and what latent causal structures remain active beneath the surface?"

Etiology turns the past from a static record into a living diagnostic instrument.

Core Capabilities

/01 Causal Graph Construction

Continuous Reconstruction

Continuous or on-demand causal graph construction from heterogeneous data sources — events, logs, sensor feeds, human reports, and external intelligence — maintaining a living model of the operational environment.

/02 Path Discovery

Multi-Hop Causal Tracing

Multi-hop causal path discovery with confidence scoring and alternative-history ranking. Every anomaly is traced through its ancestry, surfacing the chains that produced the present moment.

/03 Gap Detection

Surfacing the Unknown

Surfaces missing CausalNodes or low-confidence edges that require additional collection or human adjudication. Gaps in the graph are treated as operational signals, not oversights.

/04 Counterfactual Analysis

Alternative Histories

Given a CausalGraph, evaluate the effect of intervening on specific nodes and edges. Generate and rank alternative histories to understand what interventions would have altered the path.

Etiology turns the past from a static record into a living diagnostic instrument. The past is no longer opaque.

How Etiology Works

/01

Ingest & Reconstruct

Heterogeneous data sources are continuously ingested. Events, decisions, logistics movements, and observations are mapped into CausalNodes within the operational graph.

/02

Trace & Score

Multi-hop causal paths are discovered and scored for confidence. Alternative histories are generated and ranked. Gaps in the causal graph are surfaced for further investigation.

/03

Analyze & Act

Counterfactual analysis evaluates interventions. Forensic replay reconstructs the state of the graph at any prior timestamp. Results feed into Teleology for forward-looking purpose modeling.
