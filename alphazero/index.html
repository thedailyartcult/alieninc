How a single decision becomes a thousand

01 — Start from what happened
Alpha Zero takes a decision with a real record — a trade, a stock call, a launch date — not a hypothetical. The record is the input, not a guess about it.

02 — Fork at every decision point
Each moment where a different call could have been made becomes a branch point — a fork the model is free to take differently than history did.

03 — Run it forward, millions of times
Each branch plays out independently, scored against the same measurable, real-world benchmark as what actually happened — price, volume, volatility.

04 — Surface the branch that won
Not a forecast — a ranked map of every path that could have been taken, and exactly how far the real outcome landed from the best one.

How to use

Start simulating in four steps

01 — Click "Run a Simulation"
Every button labelled Run a Simulation opens the live dashboard. Nothing to install — the simulation runs on our server, not in your browser.

02 — Set your starting point
Name, age, birthplace, savings, and a portfolio strategy. The pre-filled defaults are a fine first run.

03 — Click "Run Multiverse"
The engine spins up your chosen number of parallel lives (100 by default), ages each one year by year, and draws the branch map of where they all land.

04 — Explore the tabs
Compare portfolio strategies, read a life timeline, then ask the Financial Advisor, Health Coach, or Mentor about the life it simulated.

What it can and can't tell you

What it can't do

Can't branch off an event that was never recorded — no data, no fork.

Can't account for a genuine black-swan event; there's no prior branch to learn from.

Never reports certainty — only a distribution of outcomes and how tightly they cluster.

What we measure

Outcome delta — The gap between the best branch found and what actually happened.

Convergence — How many independent branches land on the same answer.

Backtest accuracy — Hit rate against decisions we already know the ending to.
