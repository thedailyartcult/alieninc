Founder / Alien.Inc

ALIEN was always NEIL A.

Neil Armstrong was born August 5, 1930. I was born exactly 72 years later, on August 5, 2002. He was the first man to become an extraterrestrial being — the first to step foot on a place no human belonged. A bond between aliens and humans that's never been broken since. I like to think that coincidence has some bearing on how this company and my scientific life turned out. Then again, in the 72 years between his birth and mine, I estimate that about 20 million people were born on that same day. None of them are running a seven-company conglomerate called Alien.Inc.

The founder becomes visible.

Patrick Neil A., is a co-founder and the chief executive officer of Alien.Inc Technologies. He pursued a degree in Computer Science at the University of Makati, during which he represented the National Capital Region at the Regional Festival of Talents (RFOT) in the computer systems competitions, alongside a track record in robotics and programming competitions. He later chose to drop out to establish an advanced digital infrastructure ecosystem dedicated to next-generation systems architecture, attack surface vulnerability, and high-performance vulnerability management frameworks.

His technical foundation predates his own coursework. His mother was part of the second graduating batch of computer science majors from the University of Makati — then Pamantasan ng Makati — and a scholar under the Binay administration in what is now the country's wealthiest city. Much of what he carries into systems architecture, he inherited from her.

She spent fifteen years as Business Operating System Control at M Lhuillier's headquarters at Heart Tower 108 Valero Street, Salcedo Village, reporting directly to Charles Lhuillier (Executive Vice President at M. Lhuillier), before moving through other domains and eventually retiring into private life.
