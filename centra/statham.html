How It Works

1

Check Your Balance

Your account balance is shown in Engine Status above. Funds are pre-paid. To add funds, visit naizop.com.

2

Browse Services

Switch to the Services tab. Each service has an ID, category, rate, and min/max quantity. Note the service ID you need.

3

Place an Order

Go to Order, enter the service ID, paste the target URL, set quantity, and submit. Charge is calculated from the service rate.

4

Track Delivery

Use Tracking to check order status in real time. Orders progress: Pending → In progress → Completed.

5

Drip-Feed Orders

Set Runs and Interval for gradual delivery. Example: 1000 likes, 10 runs, 30min interval = ~100 likes every 30 min over 5 hours.

6

Use AI Automation

AI Automate lets you describe your goal in plain language. The AI selects services, calculates pricing, and places orders. Review the plan before execution or run fully autonomous.

GET /api/smm/status/{orderId}
Returns: { "charge", "start_count", "current_count", "status", "remains" }
Refill
POST /api/smm/refill?order={id}
Refill a completed order (if service supports it).
Cancel

Cancel an in-progress order.
