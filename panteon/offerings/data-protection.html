Data Protection
at Every Layer

Enterprise-grade security from the data model to the presentation layer. Column-level access controls, cryptographic marking, and zero-trust architecture protect your most sensitive information.

Security Architecture

/01 Access Control

Column-Level Security

Every field in your data model can have unique access rules. Role-based, attribute-based, and context-aware policies ensure users see only what they're authorized to see — down to individual cells.

/02 Data Marking

Classification & Marking

Automatically classify and mark data by sensitivity level. Markings propagate through every pipeline, transformation, and export — ensuring compliance follows data everywhere it flows.

/03 Audit & Lineage

Complete Audit Trail

Every data access, transformation, and export is logged with full provenance. Immutable audit trails that satisfy the most demanding regulatory and security requirements.

/04 Encryption

End-to-End Encryption

Data encrypted at rest and in transit with customer-managed keys. Support for hardware security modules (HSMs), bring-your-own-key (BYOK), and envelope encryption.

Data protection isn't a feature you bolt on. It's an architectural principle that must be woven into every layer of the platform — from ingestion to insight.
