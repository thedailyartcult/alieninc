Group Operating System

How we work

Six rules govern every company, every board pack, and every internal transaction.

01

One root, seven operators

The parent sets standards, capital discipline, and disclosure policy. Each company owns its strategy, team, and P&L. The root layer does not run the businesses.

02

Everything in writing

Tenure, service agreements, and intercompany flows are written down. Disputes go to outside arbitration, never to informal pressure.

03

One source of truth

Every dashboard, board pack, and public number reads from alieninc-ecosystem.json. If it is not in the dataset, it is not a group number.

04

Independent audit

Centra scans every property and every company against the same compliance baseline. No company is exempt, including the parent.

05

Disciplined capital

Rousseau allocates capital and reports to the board. Spending outside the plan requires an explicit allocation decision — not a favour between founders.

06

Capability transfer

KMT and Panteon are measured by what the client or company can do without them afterwards. Dependency is not a business model.

The Seven

What each company stands for

Three values per company, and the single sentence that describes how that company actually operates.

7 Companies
01 / SOFTWARE AND AI
Enterprise platforms

Panteon

Ontology-driven business operating systems, edge AI, and digital twins for the public, private, and nonprofit sectors.

Site
  • Ontology before automation
    Model the business correctly first. Automation bolted onto a bad model only makes the error faster.
  • Own the model
    We build and host the intelligence layer ourselves. Clients are never a thin wrapper around someone else's API.
  • Outlive the demo
    A system is not delivered until it survives its first audit, its first handover, and its fifth year.
How we workSmall product teams, one shared ontology, edge-first deployments, and no feature shipped without a named operator who owns it.
02 / AEROSPACE INTELLIGENCE
Technical analysis

A-SAN

Asia's technical intelligence platform for advanced aerospace systems analysis and air superiority insights.

Site
  • Evidence over noise
    Every claim traces to a source, a platform, or a physical system. Hype is not analysis.
  • Depth, plainly stated
    The work is technical; the writing is not. We explain complex systems without hiding behind jargon.
  • Independent by default
    No vendor sponsorship and no manufacturer narrative shapes the assessment.
How we workAnalyst-led research with open sourcing, explicit uncertainty, and a standing rule that a conclusion can be overturned by better evidence.
03 / CULTURE AND MEDIA
Independent publishing

The Daily Art Cult

Independent publisher and cultural media studio for membership, editorial series, and licensing.

Site
  • The artist first
    Artists are collaborators and rights holders, not content supply. Terms are published and fair.
  • Editorial independence
    Membership funds the work, so advertisers and sponsors never set the editorial line.
  • Culture is infrastructure
    What a civilisation keeps is what it chose to preserve. Publishing is part of that preservation.
How we workMember-supported, series-driven publishing with clear licensing, credited artists, and no pay-to-play coverage.
04 / CAPITAL
Holdings and allocation

Rousseau Holdings

Capital allocation, governance, board reporting, and portfolio operating discipline.

Site
  • Mandate before capital
    Money follows a written mandate and a measurable plan. There is no allocation without a thesis.
  • Patience over velocity
    We would rather compound slowly than force a return that damages the institution.
  • Governance before growth
    Boards, reporting, and controls are installed before scale, not after the first crisis.
How we workScheduled board cycles, one consolidated dataset, written investment memos, and capital released in tranches against milestones.
05 / CRISIS AND RISK
Global response

Immanuel

End-to-end crisis and risk management: always-on intelligence, enterprise platforms, and in-country security and medical teams across 190+ countries.

Site
  • Presence, not promise
    Coverage is measured by teams on the ground, not by a partner list on a slide.
  • Duty of care
    The person in the field outranks the commercial outcome. Always.
  • Calm under pressure
    In a crisis we communicate plainly, decide quickly, and never trade in panic.
How we workAlways-on monitoring, named in-country leads, rehearsed response playbooks, and a single incident channel per client.
06 / SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE
Continuous verification

Centra

Automated security scanning, vulnerability assessment, bot defense, intrusion detection, and compliance monitoring for the entire group.

Site
  • Assume breach
    Design and test as if the perimeter is already gone. Detection matters more than denial.
  • Verify continuously
    A clean scan is a moment, not a status. Every property is re-checked on a schedule.
  • One baseline for all
    The parent, the smallest subsidiary, and every vendor meet the same standard.
How we workAutomated scanning on a fixed cadence, findings ranked by exploitability, and remediation tracked to closure by a named owner.
07 / CONSULTING
Applied AI and transformation

KMT Consulting Group

Strategy, applied AI workflow transformation, operating model design, and post-merger integration.

Site
  • Strategy that ships
    An engagement ends with a working workflow and an owner, not a deck and a recommendation.
  • Transfer, don't depend
    We build capability inside the client team. A permanent dependency is a failed engagement.
  • Clarity after merger
    Post-merger work is judged by how few decisions remain ambiguous after ninety days.
How we workEmbedded operators, short delivery cycles, measurable workflow outcomes, and an explicit exit plan agreed at kickoff.
