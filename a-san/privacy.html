Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

A-SAN (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy describes what data we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using A-SAN, you agree to the practices described below.

Data we collect

Public browsing

When you browse the public catalog, we log standard request metadata (IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamp) for security and analytics purposes. No account is required.

Access requests

When you submit the access request form, we collect the information you provide: name, email address, organization, role, and stated purpose. This information is used solely to evaluate and process your access request.

Verified partner accounts

Verified partners may have account credentials and usage data associated with their sessions. We log search queries and page views to improve the platform and for security auditing.

How we use data

  • To process and manage access requests.
  • To provide and improve the platform's features and content.
  • To monitor for abuse, unauthorized access, and security threats.
  • To compile aggregate, anonymized analytics about platform usage.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We do not use your data to train external machine learning models.

Data retention

Access request data is retained for the duration of the review process plus 12 months. Browsing logs are retained for 90 days. Verified partner account data is retained for the life of the account and deleted within 30 days of account closure.

Your rights

You may request a copy of your personal data, request correction or deletion, or withdraw consent for processing at any time. To exercise these rights, contact the A-SAN team via the access page.

Security

A-SAN employs industry-standard security measures including encrypted transit (TLS), access controls, and audit logging. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data using commercially acceptable means.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of A-SAN after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy or your data can be directed to the A-SAN team through the access request page.
