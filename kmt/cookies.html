Cookies Policy

Last updated (modified): 17 July 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, provide information to site owners, and improve user experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: Required for website functionality, session management, and security. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our knowledge resources and service descriptions.
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings, cookie consent preferences, and display choices.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set cookies on your device, including analytics and infrastructure providers. These third parties have their own privacy policies and we do not control third-party cookies.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies. You can also use our cookie consent banner to accept or reject non-essential cookies. Note: blocking essential cookies may impair website functionality.

6. Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our website, a cookie consent banner will appear at the bottom of the page. This banner allows you to accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or learn more about our cookie practices. Your consent preferences are stored for 12 months.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
