1. Who We Are

1609 Holdings ("we", "us", or "our") is a holding company within the Alien Inc group of companies, domiciled in the United Kingdom. 1609 Holdings functions as the capital allocator and strategic coordinator for the consolidated group, overseeing real estate acquisitions, maritime assets, investment portfolio management, and subsidiary governance.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and process your personal data when you visit our website, invest in our funds, or otherwise interact with our services (the "Services"). This Privacy Policy does not apply to data we process on behalf of enterprise customers of our subsidiaries' business offerings.

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR and EU GDPR), the data controller is:

1609 Holdings
Data Protection Officer
Email: privacy@1609holdings.com
London, United Kingdom

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

Data you provide to us directly

  • Identity and Contact Data: Your name, title, email address, phone number, postal address, and professional affiliation when you register for updates, submit an investment inquiry, or communicate with us.
  • Investment and Financial Data: If you invest through our fund structures, we collect subscription documents, KYC (Know Your Customer) documentation, accreditation status, tax identification numbers, bank account details, and investment history. This data is required under anti-money laundering regulations and securities law.
  • Communication Data: Correspondence you send us, including emails, contact form submissions, and records of meetings or calls with our team.
  • Preferences: Your marketing and communication preferences, including whether you wish to receive investor updates, sector reports, or event invitations.

Data we collect automatically

  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, time zone setting, and platform type when you access our website.
  • Usage Information: Pages visited, time spent on pages, navigation paths, referring URLs, and interaction patterns on our website.
  • Cookie Information: Information collected through cookies and similar tracking technologies. For details, see Section 13 ("Cookies").

Data from third-party sources

  • Public Registers and Databases: We may verify your identity against government-issued sanctions lists, PEP (Politically Exposed Persons) databases, and corporate registry records as required by anti-money laundering legislation.
  • Referral Sources: If you are introduced to us by an intermediary, we may receive your name and contact details from that party.
  • Service Providers: We may receive analytical and technical data from our website analytics providers.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and facilitate the investment products and services offered through 1609 Holdings;
  • To evaluate and process investment inquiries and subscription requests;
  • To perform KYC/AML due diligence as required by applicable financial regulations;
  • To administer your investor account and provide investor reporting;
  • To communicate with you, including sending investor updates, sector insights, and event invitations;
  • To manage our website and improve user experience;
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including tax reporting, anti-money laundering requirements, and securities regulation;
  • To enforce our terms of service and protect our legal rights;
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, unauthorized access, or other illegal activity;
  • To conduct internal analytics and research on investor engagement and service improvement.

5. Data Recipients and Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may disclose your personal data to the following categories of recipients:

  • Group Companies: Other entities within the Alien Inc group (including Exosphere, Hawksight, Statute & Precedent, The Daily Art Cult, and St. Alcantara Foundation) where necessary for consolidated reporting, strategic coordination, or operational purposes. Our group companies are bound by intra-group data processing agreements.
  • Service Providers: Third-party providers who assist us in operating our business, including: website hosting and maintenance, cloud infrastructure, email communications, analytics, legal and accounting services, and KYC/AML screening providers. These providers are bound by data processing agreements and may only process data on our instructions.
  • Financial Institutions: Banks, custodians, and administrators necessary for the processing of investment transactions and fund operations.
  • Regulatory and Tax Authorities: HMRC, the FCA, and other regulatory bodies where required by law or regulation.
  • Legal and Professional Advisors: Our lawyers, auditors, and professional consultants where necessary for the provision of their services.
  • Law Enforcement: Where required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of 1609 Holdings, our investors, or others.

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your personal data may be transferred as part of the transaction. We will notify you of any such change and any choices you may have regarding your data.

6. International Data Transfers

1609 Holdings operates across international jurisdictions. When you access our Services from outside the United Kingdom, your personal data may be transferred to our servers or to servers of our service providers in other countries.

Where we transfer personal data outside the UK or the European Economic Area (EEA), we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Adequacy Decisions: Transfers to countries that the UK Secretary of State or the European Commission has determined provide an adequate level of data protection.
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): The International Data Transfer Addendum or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, as applicable, approved under Article 46 of the UK GDPR or EU GDPR.
  • Binding Corporate Rules: Where applicable, intra-group transfers are governed by binding corporate rules approved by the relevant supervisory authority.

If you have questions about the safeguards we apply to international transfers, you may contact us at privacy@1609holdings.com.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. The specific retention periods are as follows:

  • Investor Data: Duration of the investment relationship plus 7 years (regulatory requirement).
  • KYC/AML Documentation: 5 years from the end of the business relationship, in accordance with the Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Transfer of Funds (Information on the Payer) Regulations 2017.
  • Contact Inquiry Data: 24 months from the date of last interaction.
  • Marketing Preferences: Until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe.
  • Website Analytics Data: 26 months from collection.
  • Communication Records: 6 years from the date of communication.

When personal data is no longer required, we will securely delete or anonymize it in accordance with our data retention policy.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable data protection laws, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access (Article 15): Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification (Article 16): Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure (Article 17): Request deletion of your personal data, subject to our legal and regulatory retention obligations.
  • Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18): Request that we limit how we process your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability (Article 20): Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Article 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including for direct marketing purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@1609holdings.com. We will respond to your request within the period required by applicable data protection law.

9. Right to Withdraw Consent

Where our processing of your personal data is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us at privacy@1609holdings.com or by using the unsubscribe link in our marketing communications.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. Where consent is withdrawn, we will cease processing your data for the relevant purpose, subject to any other legal basis for processing that may apply.

10. Statutory and Contractual Requirements

Where we request personal data from you, we will inform you at the time of collection whether the provision of your data is a statutory or contractual requirement, or a requirement necessary to enter into a contract, as well as the possible consequences of not providing the data (Article 13(2)(e) GDPR).

For example:

  • Investment Subscriptions: KYC documentation is a statutory requirement under anti-money laundering regulations. Failure to provide this data will result in inability to process your investment subscription.
  • Contact Inquiries: Providing your email address is necessary to respond to your inquiry. Failure to provide this data will result in inability to respond.

11. Automated Decision-Making

We do not use your personal data for automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you (Article 22 GDPR).

Where we use automated systems for KYC screening (such as sanctions list checks), these are used solely to flag potential matches for human review and do not constitute solely automated decision-making with legal effect.

12. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest;
  • Access controls and authentication mechanisms;
  • Regular security assessments and penetration testing;
  • Employee training on data protection and information security;
  • Incident response procedures for data breaches.

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours and, where required, notify you without undue delay.

13. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience, analyse website traffic, and personalise content. Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website.

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function correctly. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website. These are only set with your consent.
  • Preference Cookies: Allow the website to remember choices you make to provide enhanced functionality.

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings or through our cookie consent banner. For more details, please refer to our Cookies Policy.
