Our Sale Process

We are firm believers that there are no shortcuts to a successful sale. On average, it takes 6-12 months from when we are engaged to sell a business.

We close over 95% of the transactions we work on because we follow a proven process and drive value by ensuring the information is accurately presented, your story is told in the right way, we only work with qualified buyers, and we remain involved throughout the closing process.

Planning & Data Collection

We identify your sale objectives and clearly understand what we need to deliver on to ensure a successful outcome. We spend 1-2 months doing a detailed review of the business’s operations and financials via several on-site & virtual meetings to prepare a financial model and prospectus describing the business and its growth potential.

Go-to-Market

Confidentiality is critical to our process; every buyer we contact is pre-approved by you. On average, we contact 60 to 100 qualified buyers and have multiple calls with them to confirm their interest and reaffirm the opportunity your business offers them.

Preliminary Offers

Our goal is to create a competitive auction to increase your exit price. We generate between 5 and 15 preliminary offers prior to the buyers meeting with you. Buyers are assessed based on financial strength, cultural fit, and ability to close. The top 3 to 5 groups are selected to advance to the next stage.

Final Offers

The top buyer groups meet with you to learn more about your business and receive additional information to firm up the bank financing required to close their offer. A second and final Letter Of Intent (LOI) is submitted. We do the heavy lifting upfront, which helps us achieve our historical +95% closing rate.

Operational Diligence

Exosphere leads the closing process jointly with your accountant and lawyer. We host weekly calls to address any friction and ensure consistent progress and data flow. Our job doesn't finish once we source an offer; we work with you until the very end.

Legals & Closing

We support the lawyer in preparing the information required for the legal documents. We also regularly communicate with the buyer’s bank to ensure all the information is readily available and complete to avoid any deal delays.

Our Pre-Sale Process

The reason why over 80% of our clients are referred by Canadian Banks is that we start working with our prospective clients 1 to 5 years in advance to provide actionable pre-sale planning advice.

Our goal is to identify and provide recommendations on how to ensure your business is best positioned for an exit. We work in harmony with your accountant, lawyer, wealth advisor and commercial banker to ensure you are exit-ready.

Assessment

We start with a business valuation and a review of your organizational chart and operations to provide feedback on pre-sale readiness.

Recommendation

Based on our experience, we have a very clear understanding of what business buyers care about. We look to identify 3-5 actionable items which will increase your exit value.

Implementation

Depending on our recommendation, Exosphere has a network of part-time CFOs, business coaches, consultants and recruiters to help implement these pre-sale recommendations.

Monitoring

While these recommendations are implemented, the Exosphere team will maintain regular contact with you to ensure you are on track and provide regular feedback on market trends affecting your sector and business valuation.

Exit Ready

Only once you are ready to start the sale process and we are aligned on it do we sign an engagement letter.
