1. Who We Are

Panteon ("we", "us", or "our") is the cybersecurity and vulnerability management division of the Alien Inc group of companies. Panteon provides automated security scanning, vulnerability assessment, bot defense, and compliance monitoring services to protect the digital infrastructure of the Alien Inc ecosystem and its partners.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and process your personal data when you visit our website, use our security platform, or otherwise interact with us (the "Services").

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the data controller is:

Panteon
Data Protection Officer
London, United Kingdom

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

Data you provide to us directly

  • Identity and Contact Data: Your name, email address, phone number, and company affiliation when you create an account, request a security assessment, or contact our support team.
  • Account Credentials: Username and encrypted password when you register for access to the Panteon security platform.
  • Security Scan Data: IP addresses, domain names, network configurations, and vulnerability data you submit for scanning. This data is processed solely to perform the security services you have requested.
  • Communication Data: Correspondence you send us, including support tickets, security incident reports, and records of communications with our team.

Data we collect automatically

  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and platform type when you access our website or security dashboard.
  • Usage Information: Pages visited, scan history, feature usage, navigation patterns, and interaction data on our platform.
  • Security Log Data: Authentication logs, access timestamps, and security event records for platform integrity and audit purposes.
  • Cookie Information: Information collected through cookies and similar technologies. For details, see Section 13 ("Cookies").

Data from third-party sources

  • Public Threat Intelligence: We collect publicly available threat intelligence data, including IP reputation databases, known vulnerability databases (CVE), and security advisories to enhance our scanning capabilities.
  • Group Companies: Security scan requests and incident data from other Alien Inc group companies for coordinated defense operations.
  • Service Providers: Analytical and technical data from our infrastructure and analytics providers.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and operate the Panteon security platform and scanning services;
  • To perform vulnerability assessments and security scans as requested;
  • To operate bot defense, honeypot traps, and intrusion detection systems;
  • To monitor and respond to security incidents across the Alien Inc ecosystem;
  • To manage your account and provide customer support;
  • To communicate with you, including security alerts, vulnerability notifications, and platform updates;
  • To improve our security algorithms and detection capabilities;
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including data protection and cybersecurity requirements;
  • To enforce our terms of service and protect our legal rights;
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate unauthorized access, attacks, or other security threats.

5. Data Recipients and Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may disclose your personal data to the following categories of recipients:

  • Group Companies: Other entities within the Alien Inc group (including Rousseau, Immanuel, The Daily Art Cult, and Alcantara Art Foundation) for coordinated security operations and threat intelligence sharing.
  • Infrastructure Providers: Cloud hosting and security infrastructure providers who assist in delivering our services, bound by data processing agreements.
  • Threat Intelligence Partners: Security organizations and ISACs (Information Sharing and Analysis Centers) for threat intelligence sharing, conducted under anonymization and aggregation protocols.
  • Regulatory Authorities: Where required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Panteon, our clients, or others.
  • Law Enforcement: Where necessary to report suspected criminal activity or respond to valid legal process.

6. International Data Transfers

Panteon operates globally to provide real-time security monitoring. When you access our Services, your personal data may be transferred to our servers or to servers of our service providers in other countries.

Where we transfer personal data outside the UK or the European Economic Area (EEA), we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Adequacy Decisions: Transfers to countries recognized as providing adequate data protection.
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): Approved under Article 46 of the UK GDPR or EU GDPR.
  • Encryption in Transit: All data transfers are encrypted using TLS 1.3 or higher.

If you have questions about international transfers, please contact us through the Panteon support portal.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. Specific retention periods include:

  • Account Data: Duration of your account plus 12 months after deletion.
  • Security Scan Data: 90 days from completion of the scan, unless required for ongoing incident response.
  • Threat Intelligence Data: Retained in anonymized form for ongoing security research.
  • Communication Records: 24 months from the date of communication.
  • Security Logs: 12 months for audit and compliance purposes.
  • Marketing Preferences: Until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe.

When personal data is no longer required, we will securely delete or anonymize it. Security scan data is permanently purged from our systems after the retention period expires.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable data protection laws, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access (Article 15): Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification (Article 16): Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure (Article 17): Request deletion of your personal data, subject to our legal and regulatory retention obligations.
  • Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18): Request that we limit how we process your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Data Portability (Article 20): Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Article 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests, including for direct marketing purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer through the Panteon support portal.

9. Right to Withdraw Consent

Where our processing of your personal data is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link in our communications or by contacting us through the Panteon support portal.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

10. Statutory and Contractual Requirements

Where we request personal data from you, we will inform you at the time of collection whether the provision of your data is a statutory or contractual requirement, or a requirement necessary to enter into a contract, as well as the possible consequences of not providing the data (Article 13(2)(e) GDPR).

  • Platform Registration: Creating an account requires your name and email address. Failure to provide this data will result in inability to access the Panteon platform.
  • Security Scans: Submitting target IP addresses or domains is necessary to perform scanning. Failure to provide this data will result in inability to perform the requested scan.

11. Automated Decision-Making

Our security scanning and threat detection systems operate using automated processes to identify vulnerabilities and security threats. These automated systems:

  • Do not produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you;
  • Are used solely to identify technical vulnerabilities in network infrastructure;
  • Are subject to human review for all actions that may affect your access or services;
  • Are designed to protect, not restrict, your digital security.

You have the right to request human intervention in any automated decision-making process that affects you (Article 22 GDPR).

12. Data Security

As a cybersecurity company, we implement industry-leading technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data:

  • End-to-end encryption for data in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256);
  • Zero-trust architecture with multi-factor authentication;
  • Regular penetration testing and security assessments;
  • SOC 2 compliant operations;
  • 24/7 security monitoring and incident response;
  • Employee security training and background checks.

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours and, where required, notify you without undue delay.

13. Cookies

Our website and platform use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience, ensure security, and analyse usage patterns. Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for platform authentication, session management, and security. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our platform. These are only set with your consent.
  • Security Cookies: Used for CSRF protection, rate limiting, and fraud prevention.

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings or our cookie consent banner. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.
