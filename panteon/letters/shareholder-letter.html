Letter to Shareholders

Our mission, our progress, and the road ahead.

July 2026

Building the Operating System for the Next Century

Panteon is the cybersecurity and AI-powered automation division of Alien Inc, a parent operating group connecting seven independent companies across cybersecurity, cultural preservation, digital media, capital allocation, crisis management, legal services, and AI consulting.

Our mission is straightforward: build software that helps organizations make better decisions, defend their operations, and deliver results in environments that cannot afford to fail. We build for the hardest problems — the ones where software is not a convenience but a necessity.

Our Platforms

Panteon operates across five domains — Terra (Land), Abyss (Sea), Stratos (Air), Cosmos (Space), and Cyber (World Wide Web). Each domain is served by a core platform designed to operate independently and converge with the others.

YONO automates operations from the factory floor to the front lines — coordinating logistics, predicting equipment failures, and optimizing deployments in real time. Babel provides AI-driven intelligence for strategy and defense operations. Apollo handles autonomous software delivery and mission system management across every environment. Spinal Craker is the ontology-powered business operating system that unifies data across organizations into a single operational picture.

Together, these platforms form an operating system that spans every domain where decisions must be made — and made quickly.

The Five Elements

We believe that modern operations — whether in defense, energy, healthcare, or critical infrastructure — are won or lost across five domains simultaneously. A decision made in one domain affects outcomes in all the others. The organizations that can reason across domains in real time will have a decisive advantage.

Panteon's architecture was designed from the ground up for this convergence. Our ontology-based data layer understands the relationships between domains, our AI models reason across datasets that span them, and our deployment infrastructure ensures that software reaches every environment — from cloud to edge to disconnected networks.

Centra: 275,000 Detection Plugins

Our cybersecurity division, Centra, has reached a significant milestone: 275,000 vulnerability detection plugins generated from NVD data covering CVEs from 2017 through 2026. This is one of the largest plugin libraries in the industry, providing automated vulnerability scanning, compliance monitoring, and threat intelligence across the Alien Inc ecosystem and beyond.

Centra's plugin library is publicly searchable at centra.alieninc.tech/plugins.html, and the scanning engine is operational across all Alien Inc properties.

The Alien Inc Ecosystem

Panteon does not operate in isolation. We are part of a group of seven companies, each focused on a distinct domain but unified by shared infrastructure, shared data, and a shared commitment to operational excellence:

Rousseau — Holding company and governance. The Daily Art Cult — Digital media and publishing. KMT Consulting Group — Strategy and applied AI consulting. St. Alcantara Foundation — Culture and art preservation. Statute & Precedent — Legal services, compliance, and governance. Immanuel — Crisis and risk management. Centra — Cybersecurity and vulnerability scanning.

This ecosystem model allows us to share data, infrastructure, and intelligence across domains that would normally operate in silos. It is the foundation of our operating model, and it is what makes the Five Elements framework possible.

The Road Ahead

We are building for the long term. The challenges we face — from great power competition to critical infrastructure defense to the responsible deployment of AI — require software that is resilient, auditable, and designed to operate in the hardest environments on Earth.

We are not optimizing for quick wins. We are building the operating system that the next century demands. And we are building it now.

Patrick Neil

CEO & Founder, Alien Inc Technologies
