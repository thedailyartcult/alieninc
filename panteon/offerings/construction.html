Project Lifecycle Intelligence

Construction projects generate massive volumes of data across dozens of systems — BIM models, schedules, procurement logs, RFIs, change orders, daily reports, and safety records. Panteon integrates all of it into a living project ontology.

Design & Planning

Integrate BIM models, geotechnical data, and permitting workflows. AI-powered clash detection and constructability analysis before ground breaks.

/02

Procurement & Supply

Track materials from order to delivery. Predict supply chain risks, qualify alternate suppliers, and optimize just-in-time logistics for congested sites.

/03

Construction Execution

Real-time progress tracking, quality inspection management, and AI-driven schedule optimization that adapts to weather, labor, and material constraints.

/04

Commissioning & Handover

Digital twin handover with complete as-built data. Link construction records to operations and maintenance systems for the asset's full lifecycle.
