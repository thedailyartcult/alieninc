1. Who We Are

Alien.Inc ("we", "us", or "our") is the parent operating group for a portfolio of seven independent companies spanning publishing, cybersecurity, capital allocation, consulting, acquisition advisory, cultural preservation, and legal operations. Our group companies include The Daily Art Cult, Panteon, 1609 Holdings, KMT Consulting Group, Exosphere, Statute & Precedent, and the St. Alcantara Foundation.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and process your personal data when you visit our website, interact with our operating companies, or otherwise engage with the Alien.Inc group (the "Services").

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data for the purposes described below:

Data you provide to us directly

  • Identity and Contact Data: Your name, email address, phone number, company affiliation, and role when you contact us, subscribe to updates, or engage with any Alien.Inc group company.
  • Communication Data: Correspondence you send us, including inquiries, partnership proposals, and records of communications with our team.
  • Operating Data: Information you provide when interacting with our operating dashboard, data feeds, or group reporting tools.

Data we collect automatically

  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and platform type when you access our website or any group company site.
  • Usage Information: Pages visited, navigation patterns, and interaction data on our root site and cross-group navigation paths.
  • Cookie Information: Information collected through cookies and similar technologies. For details, see Section 13 ("Cookies").

Data from third-party sources

  • Group Companies: Where you engage with multiple Alien.Inc group companies, we may receive data from those entities to coordinate group services.
  • Service Providers: Analytical and technical data from our infrastructure, hosting, and analytics providers.
  • Public Sources: Publicly available business data used for market insight and group strategy.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and operate the Alien.Inc group website and navigation layer;
  • To facilitate communication between you and relevant group companies;
  • To manage and respond to inquiries, partnership proposals, and service requests;
  • To operate our group-level analytics, reporting, and operating data systems;
  • To improve the functionality and user experience of our group web properties;
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations, including data protection and corporate governance requirements;
  • To enforce our terms of service and protect our legal rights;
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate security incidents across the group network.

We process your personal data for these specified purposes only and do not use it for any incompatible purpose without further notice.

5. Data Recipients and Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We may share your personal data with the following categories of recipients:

  • Group Companies: Other entities within the Alien.Inc group for coordinated operations and service delivery. When you interact with a specific group company through our root site, we may share your contact data with that company to facilitate your request.
  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting, analytics, email delivery, and security infrastructure providers who assist in delivering our services. All providers are bound by data processing agreements and confidentiality obligations.
  • Professional Advisers: Legal counsel, auditors, and compliance advisers where necessary to protect our legitimate interests or comply with legal obligations.
  • Regulatory Authorities: Where required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Alien.Inc, our group companies, or others.

We do not disclose or transfer your personal data to any recipient not listed above without providing you with advance notice and, where required, obtaining your consent.

6. International Data Transfers

Alien.Inc operates globally through its group companies and service providers. When you access our Services, your personal data may be transferred to our servers or to servers of our service providers in other countries, including outside the EEA and outside the UK.

Where we transfer personal data outside the UK or the European Economic Area (EEA), we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Adequacy Decisions: Transfers to countries recognized by the UK Government or European Commission as providing adequate data protection.
  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): Contractual clauses approved under Article 46 of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, which provide legally enforceable safeguards for your data.
  • Encryption in Transit: All data transfers are encrypted using TLS 1.3 or higher.

If you have questions about international transfers or the specific safeguards we apply, contact us at privacy@alien.inc.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law. Specific retention periods include:

  • Inquiry and Communication Data: Retained for 24 months from the date of last communication, after which it is securely deleted or anonymised.
  • Technical and Usage Data: Retained for 12 months in identifiable form, then anonymised for aggregate group analytics.
  • Security Logs: Retained for 12 months for audit and compliance purposes in accordance with applicable legal requirements.
  • Marketing Data: Retained until you withdraw consent or unsubscribe, after which your data is removed within 30 days.

When personal data is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymise it. Data that must be retained for legal or regulatory reasons is stored in accordance with our retention schedule and kept for the statutory period required by applicable law.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable data protection laws, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access (Article 15): Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you and information about how we process it.
  • Right to Rectification (Article 16): Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Erasure (Article 17): Request deletion of your personal data, subject to our legal and regulatory retention obligations.
  • Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18): Request that we limit how we process your personal data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Object (Article 21): Object to processing of your personal data based on legitimate interests, including for direct marketing purposes.
  • Right to Data Portability (Article 20): Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@alien.inc. We will respond to your request within the period required by applicable data protection law, and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

9. Right to Withdraw Consent

Where our processing of your personal data is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time. You may do so by contacting us at privacy@alien.inc or by using the unsubscribe link in our communications.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. We will honour your withdrawal of consent promptly and cease the relevant processing activity.

10. Statutory and Contractual Requirements

Where we request personal data from you, we will inform you at the time of collection whether the provision of your data is a statutory or contractual requirement, or a requirement necessary to enter into a contract, as well as the possible consequences of not providing the data (Article 13(2)(e) GDPR).

  • Contact Form Submissions: Providing your name and email address when contacting us is necessary for us to respond to your inquiry. Failure to provide this data may result in inability to receive a response.
  • Group Company Engagements: Where you enter into a contractual relationship with an Alien.Inc group company, the provision of specified data may be a contractual requirement or a requirement necessary to enter into a contract. The consequences of failure to provide such data will be communicated at the point of collection.

11. Automated Decision-Making

Alien.Inc does not engage in automated decision-making, including profiling, which produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you (Article 22 GDPR). Our operating data systems and group analytics are used for internal management purposes only and do not make decisions about individuals based solely on automated processing.

12. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures designed to protect your personal data from accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure, or access. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256);
  • Network security monitoring through our Panteon cybersecurity division;
  • Multi-factor authentication for administrative access;
  • Regular security assessments and penetration testing;
  • Incident response procedures aligned with industry best practice;
  • Employee security training and access controls.

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours and, where required, notify you without undue delay.

13. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience, ensure security, and analyse usage patterns. Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for website functionality, session management, and security. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our site. These are only set with your consent.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences to improve your browsing experience.

You can manage your cookie preferences through our cookie consent banner or your browser settings. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.
