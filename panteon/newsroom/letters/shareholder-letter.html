Letter to Shareholders

CEO Patrick Neil's annual letter on Panteon's mission, strategy, and the state of the business.

January 2026

The State of the Business

2025 was a year of extraordinary momentum for Panteon. We grew annual recurring revenue by 37% to $3.2 billion, expanded our customer base to over 580 organizations worldwide, and deepened our presence in the sectors that matter most, defense, healthcare, energy, and critical infrastructure. Our net revenue retention rate reached 122%, reflecting the indispensable role our platforms play in our customers' operations.

More importantly, we expanded the scope of what we deliver. The launch of Panteon AIP, our artificial intelligence platform, marked a fundamental shift in how enterprises deploy AI. Rather than offering AI as a standalone tool, we embedded it into the operational fabric of our customers' organizations, connecting foundation models to their data, their workflows, and their decisions.

The AI Revolution

We believe we are in the early stages of the most significant technological transformation since the advent of the internet. AI is not a feature or a product category. It is a new layer of operating system for every organization on Earth. The companies that integrate AI into their operations first, deepest, and most responsibly will define the next era of industrial and institutional capability.

Panteon AIP was built for this moment. Our ontology-based architecture gives AI models the context they need to reason about real-world operations, not just text and images. Our agent framework allows organizations to deploy autonomous AI that operates within guardrails they define. And our deployment infrastructure, through Panteon Apollo, ensures these systems can be updated, secured, and managed across any environment, from cloud to edge to disconnected battlefield networks.

In 2025, AIP was deployed across 200+ customer environments, from Fortune 500 enterprises to government agencies. The use cases range from supply chain optimization to clinical operations to defense planning. The common thread is that AIP is not being used for demos or pilots. It is running production operations at scale.

Defense and National Security

The geopolitical landscape has never been more challenging. The convergence of great power competition, regional conflicts, and emerging technological threats demands a new approach to defense software. The side that makes better decisions faster wins. Software is the decisive advantage.

Panteon's role in national security deepened significantly in 2025. Our Gotham platform is now operational across all combatant commands, supporting mission planning, intelligence fusion, and logistics optimization. The American Tech Fellowship program placed 140 engineers inside government agencies, building production systems that directly support national security missions.

We believe the most important thing a technology company can do for its country is to build the best possible tools for those who defend it. We take that responsibility seriously and will continue to invest in the cleared engineering talent, security infrastructure, and mission understanding required to serve those who serve us.

Our Mission

Panteon exists to help the world's most important organizations turn their data into operational advantage. We build software for the hardest problems, for the institutions that cannot afford to fail. Our customers include the world's largest aerospace companies, health systems, energy providers, financial institutions, and defense organizations.

We are not a company that optimizes for the easy problems or the quick wins. We are built for the long term, for the complex missions, for the institutions that shape the world. Our responsibility to our shareholders is matched only by our responsibility to the men and women who depend on our software to make the decisions that matter most.

We enter 2026 with conviction in our mission, confidence in our platforms, and gratitude for the trust our customers and shareholders place in us every day.

Patrick Neil

Chief Executive Officer, Panteon Technologies Inc.
