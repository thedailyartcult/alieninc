/ 02 — DESIGN LANGUAGE

Built on index.html, not beside it

Tokens, header, mountain canvas, reveal animations, stacked buttons, and footer are cloned from the canonical Panteon homepage (index.html:35-52 tokens, index.html:541-568 reveal). The arena uses JetBrains Mono at clamp(1.05rem,2.1vw,1.35rem) for optimal legibility at speed.
