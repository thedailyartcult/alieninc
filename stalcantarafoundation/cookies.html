What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website.

How We Use Cookies

We use essential cookies for website operation and security. A cookie consent banner appears on first visit with accept and reject options. Cookie types: session (end of session), security (24h), analytics (12 months), preferences (12 months).

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through browser settings or our cookie consent banner. See our Privacy Policy for more details.
