Overview

Centra is a software company that builds the world's leading software for data-driven operations and decision-making. For more than a decade, we’ve worked with customers in the most secure and highly-regulated industries and built software for their most sensitive data. Today, security remains the cornerstone of our product development, company culture, and internal operations.

Centra cares deeply about the security outcomes of our customers, and we’re committed to transparency about our security practices and program. We stand resolute in continuously improving our security, data protection, and privacy controls to give you the most effective means of protecting your data possible.

Axios Breach Bulletin

April 9, 2026
General

We have conducted a thorough investigation and validated that we are not impacted by the Axios breach. The malicious Axios package was never published to any Centra production environments.

Centra Security Bulletin - CENTRASEC-2025-52

April 7, 2026
Vulnerabilities

Bulletin ID: CENTRASEC-2025-52 | CVE: CVE-2025-68427

Summary: Object Security Policies configured on Pipeline Builder-backed object types were being silently deleted during pipeline deployments on Foundry stacks. This could have resulted in unintended data exposure where manually configured row-level security restrictions were removed without warning. The affected service has been patched and automatically deployed to all Apollo-managed Centra instances.

Trivy Breach Bulletin

March 27, 2026
General

Centra has been tracking the compromise of Aqua Security’s Trivy supply chain. We have evaluated all links to the vulnerable version across the entire Centra developer landscape and confirmed no compromise. Centra has frozen our CVS scanning engine on the last known safe version of Trivy.

If you think you may have discovered a vulnerability, please send us a note.
