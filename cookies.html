Cookies Policy

Last updated: 17 July 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, to provide information to the site owners, and to improve the user experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function properly, including session management, security, and load balancing. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our site, which pages are most visited, and how users navigate across the Alien.Inc group network.
  • Preference cookies: Remember your settings, cookie consent preferences, and display choices to enhance your browsing experience.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set cookies on your device, including analytics and cloud infrastructure providers. These third parties process data according to their own privacy policies. We do not control or have access to third-party cookies placed by external services.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies. You can also use our cookie consent banner to accept or reject non-essential cookies when you first visit our site. Note: blocking essential cookies may impair website functionality and security features.

To opt out of being tracked by Google Analytics across all websites, visit Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our website, a cookie consent banner will appear at the bottom of the page. This banner allows you to accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or learn more about our cookie practices. Your consent preferences are stored and respected for 12 months. You can change your preferences at any time by clearing your browser cookies, which will cause the consent banner to reappear on your next visit.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
