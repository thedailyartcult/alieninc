What erasure covers

Under Section 8 of our Privacy Policy you have a right to erasure (GDPR Article 17) — and California residents have the equivalent right to delete. When it applies, we securely delete or anonymise:

  • Identity, contact, and communication data — what you sent us, and records of our correspondence (Section 2);
  • Marketing data — removed within 30 days of withdrawal of consent (Section 7);
  • Technical and usage data in identifiable form, where still held.

What we may have to keep: some data must be retained for legal or regulatory reasons (Section 7) — e.g. security logs kept 12 months for audit and compliance, or records needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. Where erasure cannot fully apply, we restrict processing instead and explain why in our reply. Anonymised aggregate analytics (which no longer identifies you) is not personal data and is unaffected.
