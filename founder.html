Founder / Alien.Inc

ALIEN was always NEIL A.

Neil Armstrong was born August 5, 1930. I was born exactly 72 years later, on August 5, 2002. He was the first man to become an extraterrestrial being — the first to step foot on a place no human belonged. A bond between aliens and humans that's never been broken since. I like to think that coincidence has some bearing on how this company and my scientific life turned out. Then again, in the 72 years between his birth and mine, I estimate that about 20 million people were born on that same day. None of them are running a seven-company conglomerate called Alien.Inc.

The founder becomes visible.

Patrick Neil Alcantara (born August 5) is a Philippine founder and systems designer, known as the operator of ALIEN.INC — the parent company of a seven-company network spanning publishing, cybersecurity, identity infrastructure, and long-horizon capital. He began with The Daily Art Cult, a philosophical publishing platform, before building the technology stack that would run the rest of the network. Alcantara has noted that sharing a birth date with Neil Armstrong bears some relation to how the company took its name, though he has been careful not to overstate the connection.

Neil A. is Patrick Neil Alcantara — Neil, his middle name, and A., his surname, compressed into the identity ALIEN.INC operates under.

Every company in the network traces back to a single reference point: KMT 184.05, the coordinate given to a reference point for a unified identity, adapted into a new form every decade for four centuries. Alcantara has cited the shape of that narrative as a working model for how the network is structured.
