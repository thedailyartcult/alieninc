The Challenge

Federal health agencies struggle with fragmented data systems that impede care coordination and decision-making. Concordance needed a platform to integrate clinical, operational, and administrative data across multiple VA medical centers and public health programs.

The Solution

Built on Panteon Foundry, Concordance deployed an integrated health data platform that connects EHR systems, claims data, pharmacy records, and public health registries. AI-powered analytics support population health management, clinical research, and operational decision-making for care providers.

Panteon enables us to deliver what federal health agencies need most — a single source of truth that connects every data point to patient outcomes.
