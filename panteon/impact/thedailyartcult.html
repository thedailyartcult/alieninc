The Mission

The Daily Art Cult produces bespoke audiobooks, immersive listening experiences, and philosophical reflections. Every person who has thought deeply deserves to be heard after they are gone. The subscription funds this work — not shareholders, not a platform, the mission alone. The long-term goal: an Immortality AI that preserves a person's philosophical voice, trained on their own words, capable of responding in their own register.

The Platform

The Daily Art Cult is the founding tenant of Panteon. Spinal Craker provides the data backbone — the Ontology of patrons, publishers, reflections, and gift cards. ONO provides the intelligence — composing philosophical scripts via Gemini, synthesizing audio via Azure TTS, and automating daily delivery to every active patron.
