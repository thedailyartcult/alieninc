/ 01 — HOW IT WORKS

Monkeytype mechanics, Panteon skin

We reimplement the core loop from monkeytype without embedding it: live errors, smooth caret, word-by-word validation, and WPM = (correct chars ÷ 5) ÷ minutes. Punctuation and numbers are injected at generation time — same as Monkeytype's funbox pipeline.

  • Time, words, and quote modes
  • Live WPM / accuracy / raw
  • Restart with tab + enter
/ 02 — DESIGN LANGUAGE

Built on index.html, not beside it

Tokens, header, mountain canvas, reveal animations, stacked buttons, and footer are cloned from the canonical Panteon homepage (index.html:35-52 tokens, index.html:541-568 reveal). The arena uses JetBrains Mono at clamp(1.05rem,2.1vw,1.35rem) for optimal legibility at speed.

  • Glass cards: rgba(255,255,255,0.88) blur(10px)
  • Accent #DFF140 for caret & focus
  • No new colors or fonts
