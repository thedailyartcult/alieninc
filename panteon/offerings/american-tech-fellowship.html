American Tech Fellowship

Bridging the gap between America's top technical talent and the missions that matter most — embedding engineers directly into government agencies to deliver mission-critical software.

The Program

The American Tech Fellowship recruits world-class engineers, designers, and operators from America's leading technology companies and places them directly inside government agencies. Fellows work alongside civil servants and warfighters to build the software systems that defend national security and strengthen democratic institutions.

/01 Embed

Embedded in Mission

Fellows are placed directly within agencies — Defense, Intelligence, Health, Energy — working side-by-side with mission owners to solve their hardest technical challenges.

/02 Build

Ship Real Software

No slide decks. No strategy exercises. Fellows deploy production systems on Panteon's platform that deliver measurable impact within their first 90 days.

/03 Transform

Lasting Institutional Change

Fellows leave behind not just software, but new capabilities — training government teams to maintain, extend, and build on what was delivered.

The best engineers in America shouldn't have to choose between a career in technology and a career in service. The Fellowship makes both possible.

Fellowship Timeline

Application & Selection

Rigorous technical and mission-fit evaluation. Cohorts selected from thousands of applicants across engineering, design, and operations.

Mission Onboarding

Intensive orientation on government operations, security protocols, and Panteon platform mastery before agency embedding begins.

Agency Deployment

12-month embedded tour within a partner agency. Fellows operate as full members of the mission team with direct access to decision-makers.

Transition & Continuation

Fellows transition projects to government teams. Many continue as full-time Panteon engineers or return to industry as mission-aligned leaders.
