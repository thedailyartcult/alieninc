Modern Slavery and Human Trafficking Statement

Panteon Technologies Inc.'s statement on actions taken to prevent modern slavery and human trafficking in our operations and supply chain.

Statement Overview

Panteon Technologies Inc. is committed to preventing modern slavery and human trafficking in all forms. This statement is made pursuant to Section 54 of the UK Modern Slavery Act 2015 and sets out the steps taken during the financial year ending December 31, 2025 to ensure that slavery and human trafficking are not taking place in any of our supply chains or in any part of our own business.

Our Business

Panteon Technologies Inc. is a global software company headquartered in the United States, with operations across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. We develop and deploy enterprise software platforms for data integration, artificial intelligence, and operational decision-making. Our customers span government, defense, healthcare, energy, financial services, and technology sectors. As a software company, our direct workforce consists primarily of engineers, designers, operations specialists, and commercial teams.

Our Supply Chain

Our supply chain is primarily composed of technology services and infrastructure providers, including cloud computing platforms, data center operations, professional services contractors, and office facility management. We recognize that even in technology-focused supply chains, there is a risk of labor exploitation, particularly in facilities management, cleaning services, and hardware manufacturing. We maintain supplier standards that require compliance with all applicable labor laws and prohibit forced labor, child labor, and human trafficking.

Policies

Panteon maintains internal policies that reflect our commitment to ethical business practices. Our Code of Business Conduct and Ethics explicitly prohibits the use of forced, bonded, or involuntary labor. Our Supplier Code of Conduct requires all suppliers to comply with applicable labor laws and to provide safe, fair working conditions. We conduct annual training for procurement and management teams on modern slavery risks and reporting obligations.

Due Diligence

We have implemented a risk-based approach to supplier due diligence. High-risk categories, including facilities management and hardware procurement, are subject to enhanced review. We require suppliers to certify compliance with our Supplier Code of Conduct and reserve the right to audit supplier practices. We maintain a confidential reporting mechanism for employees and third parties to report concerns about labor practices.

Effectiveness

We measure the effectiveness of our modern slavery program through supplier compliance rates, training completion metrics, and the number of concerns raised through our reporting mechanisms. During the reporting period, no incidents of modern slavery or human trafficking were identified in our operations or supply chains. We continue to enhance our due diligence processes and supplier engagement to strengthen our prevention efforts.

This statement has been approved by the Board of Directors of Panteon Technologies Inc.

This statement is made pursuant to the UK Modern Slavery Act 2015 and covers the financial year ending December 31, 2025. Approved by the Board of Directors, January 2026.
