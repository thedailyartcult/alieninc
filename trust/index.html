Overview

Centra is the security and vulnerability scanning division of Alien Inc, a parent operating group connecting seven independent companies across cybersecurity, cultural preservation, digital media, capital allocation, crisis management, legal services, and AI consulting.

Centra continuously audits all Alien Inc properties against CIS NGINX, DISA STIG, PCI-DSS, OWASP ASVS, NIST 800-53, and NIST CSF 2.0. Our 26-plugin scanner runs automated checks across the full ecosystem, verifying infrastructure hardening, data protection, privacy compliance, and accessibility standards.

Security remains the cornerstone of our product development, company culture, and internal operations. This portal provides transparent, real-time evidence of our security posture.
