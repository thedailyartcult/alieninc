Access

A-SAN offers tiered access levels. Public visitors can browse the catalog overview; verified defense companies, researchers, and authorized partners gain access to full technical profiles, drawings, and research tools.

Access Tiers

Public
All visitors, no registration
  • Browse catalog categories
  • View equipment overviews
  • Search and filter by category
  • Basic specifications teaser
Verified Partner
Defense companies, researchers, authorized partners
  • Full technical specifications
  • Component analysis & breakdowns
  • Technical drawings & blueprints
  • Cross-platform comparison tools
  • Citation trails & source verification
  • Offline analysis requests
  • Versioned history access
