Privacy and Civil Liberties

Our Privacy and Civil Liberties team ensures that Panteon's technology respects individual rights while enabling organizations to solve their hardest problems.

Our Commitment

/01 Principles

Privacy by Design

Privacy by design, data minimization, purpose limitation, and individual rights. Our principles guide every product decision from architecture to deployment.

/02 Governance

Independent Oversight

An independent PCL team with authority to review, audit, and veto uses of our technology that risk individual rights. Reporting directly to the Board.

/03 Thought Leadership

AI Ethics and Policy

Research and policy contributions on AI ethics, data protection, and the intersection of technology and civil liberties in democratic societies.

/04 Transparency

Public Reporting

Regular transparency reports on government requests, data practices, and the ethical frameworks guiding our technology deployments.

Technology that serves humanity must respect the rights of every individual it touches.
