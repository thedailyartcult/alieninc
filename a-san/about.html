About A-SAN

ASEAN Superiority Aerospace Navigator — a technical intelligence platform for advanced aerospace and military systems analysis.

Mission

A-SAN provides defense companies, researchers, and authorized partners with structured technical intelligence on aerospace and military platforms. The platform consolidates open-source technical data, verified imagery, and analysis tools into a single searchable catalog — enabling faster research, better engineering decisions, and deeper understanding of advanced systems.

What A-SAN covers

The catalog spans ten equipment categories, from manned aircraft and unmanned aerial vehicles to missile systems, electronic warfare assets, and ground vehicles. Each entry follows a standardized ontology that makes cross-platform comparison and structured querying possible.

  • Aircraft — manned fixed-wing and rotary-wing combat and support platforms
  • UAVs — reconnaissance, attack, and loitering unmanned aerial systems
  • Air-launched munitions — air-to-air, air-to-ground munitions and guided bombs
  • Rocket and missile weapons — ballistic, cruise, and tactical missile systems
  • Sea-launched cruise missiles — naval cruise missile platforms
  • EW assets — electronic warfare, jammers, and signal intelligence
  • UGVs — unmanned ground vehicles for combat, logistics, and reconnaissance
  • Armored vehicles and equipment — main battle tanks, IFVs, APCs
  • Automotive vehicles — military trucks, logistics, and support vehicles
  • Small arms — infantry weapons and related systems

Data principles

Every entry in A-SAN is built from publicly available sources, cross-referenced for accuracy, and tagged with a confidence level. We maintain a full citation trail so that any claim can be independently verified. Corrections and updates are versioned — prior data remains accessible for longitudinal research.

Who A-SAN is for

A-SAN is designed for defense engineers, technical researchers, analysts, and organizations working on countermeasures, platform analysis, and threat assessment. Public visitors can browse the catalog overview; verified partners gain access to full technical profiles, drawings, and research tools.
